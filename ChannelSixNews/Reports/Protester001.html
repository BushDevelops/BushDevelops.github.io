Civilian Protesters Annex Facility

Yesterday at approx. 11:55 PM, our journalists were outside the facility when we heard a code red blare, and found protesters raiding the facility with zero weapons. The protesters went off to a classified area we are not at liberty to speak of, and came out with a car packed with protesters, which appeared to be the Facility Director’s limousine. Speculation of what happened is still unknown, and how they were not shot on site.
