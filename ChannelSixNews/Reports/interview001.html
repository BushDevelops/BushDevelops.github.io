Topic: Rebellion

An interview with the rebellion: are they that bad?

Channel Six News spoke with a member of the Rebellion, Maximus, about the group's origins, its motivations, and what it hopes to accomplish. Here is our full conversation log:

Channel Six: To start, could you tell us a little about the Rebellion and what it is?

Maximus: “The Rebellion was founded to stop the NBTF from experimenting on people. What really set us off was that we found people missing from their homes, and traces of NBTF left behind. That's how we knew it was them.”

Maximus says the group was formed in response to alleged experiments conducted on civilians by the NBTF.

Channel Six: What inspired you to join the rebellion?

Maximus: “I've had missing loved ones myself, so I joined to find them, and if not, bring justice to the NBTF.”

For Maximus, the conflict is personal. Like many others, his decision to join was driven by the disappearance of loved ones.

Channel Six: What do you think people misunderstand about the rebellion?

Maximus: “I think people misunderstand us for monsters or terrorists, which we are not.”

Maximus says the group is often unfairly villainized, rather than seen as people fighting for justice.

Channel Six: What are you hoping to accomplish going forward?

Maximus: “To stop the NBTF once and for all.”

Looking forward: The Rebellion's primary objective remains clear: to shut down the NBTF permanently.
