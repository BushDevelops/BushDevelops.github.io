Channel Six News spoke with the Faclity director of the NBTF, about the group's origins, its motivations, and what it hopes to accomplish. Here is our full conversation log:

The Facility's stated purpose is to create energy for humanity to use for years to come.

Channel Six:What inspired you to direct the Facility?

Facility Director: "Programming, engineering, and leading people.”

The Director says their interest in programming, engineering, and leadership inspired them to take on the role.

Channel Six: Recently, the facility has been exposed to an ongoing rebellion, do you have anything to say to that?

Facility Director: “Well, what I have to say is that I actually want to meet the demands of the civilians, the public, and the rebels, for I want peace.”

The Director says they want to meet the demands of the public and the rebels in an effort to bring an end to the ongoing conflict.

Channel Six: What do you think people misunderstand about the NBTF?

Facility Director: “I think people think we just like having a reason to kill people.”

The Director says the Facility is often misunderstood as an organization that simply wants a reason to use violence.
