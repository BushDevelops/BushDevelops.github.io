Topic: NBTF

An interview with the NBTF: STF [REDACTED]

Channel Six News spoke with STF [REDACTED] of the Special Task Force about their purpose within the Facility, operations around the core, and recent incidents. Here is our full conversation log:

Channel Six: So, what is the military's purpose in the NBTF?

STF [REDACTED]: “We are hired to protect the interests of the Facility, namely by providing a more capable response force to resistance threats within the Facility, especially around the core.”

The military's primary role is to protect the Facility and respond to resistance threats, particularly those involving the core.

Channel Six: What is it like inside of the Facility?

STF [REDACTED]: “It's honestly... don't tell the Facility executives I said this... it's a little crazy. We have rebels running around, and people keep getting sick. That's why it's mandated to carry RadAway.”

The military member describes the Facility as dangerous, citing rebel activity and radiation-related illnesses among personnel.

Channel Six: What is the purpose of the Special Task Force specifically?

STF [REDACTED]: “We are the most elite fighters of the military, and we are meant for quick and effective response and neutralization. I've seen... some things in my time.”

The Special Task Force is described as an elite unit intended to respond quickly to serious threats within the Facility.

Channel Six: What is your favorite mission?

STF [REDACTED]: “My favorite mission would be when I got to plan and lead an effective assault on the core, and through teamwork and coordination, we managed to secure it and prevent a meltdown.

“The rebels had managed to disable the core safety protocols and were close to destabilizing it.”

The military member recalls leading an assault on the core after rebels allegedly disabled its safety protocols and came close to destabilizing it.

Channel Six: Anything the viewers should know?

STF [REDACTED]: “Be careful of where you step, stay away from the testing field, very far away, and don't see things as always black and white.”

The military member warns viewers about the testing field and suggests that situations inside the Facility may be more complicated than they initially appear.

Channel Six: Would you like to respond on the most recent incidents that have occurred?

STF [REDACTED]: “I'm not sure I'm allowed to, but I'll say it anyway.

“We've had multiple attempts at resistance threats within the Facility recently, with some even getting all the codes for the core... we've also had some traitors recently.

“I promise you, I am not an undercover rebel right now...”

The military member claims that recent resistance attempts have included efforts to obtain the core's access codes and says the Facility has also dealt with suspected traitors.

Channel Six: What would you like to accomplish for the NBTF in the future?

STF [REDACTED]: “I'd like to plan an effective response against rebel raids to earn my payche- I mean, end this conflict. It's been quite heavy in casualties...”

The military member says they hope to help end the conflict and reduce the casualties caused by continued fighting. Channel six news has theorised the NBTF may not be all friendly, even for the workers. We will be reporting about their pollution rates in the next report, thank you for taking the time to read this report.
